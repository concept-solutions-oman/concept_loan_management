Concept Loan Management Odoo App simplifies the tracking and management of bank loans and deposits. Users can compute dynamic amortization schedules, calculate flat rate or effective interest (computed on daily elapsed days), and post journal entries with a single click.

Required Chart of Accounts Setup:
To ensure the automated journal entries post successfully, please create and configure the following accounts:

  • 201002 Payable Account: A specific account with code 201002 is needed.
  • Deferred Interest Account: Configured as a Current Liability account.
  • Deferred Interest Suspense Account: Configured as a Current Suspense Account.

Required Chart of Accounts Setup

Make sure Account 201002 (Payable Account), Deferred Interest Account (Current Liability), and Deferred Interest Suspense Account (Current Suspense Account) exist before generating journal entries.

Configure Bank Loan Details

Specify Loan Amount, Rate Type (Flat or Effective), First Installment Date, Disbursal Date, and Duration in Months.

Interest Rate & Repayment Calculation details:

  • Flat Rate: When Flat Rate is selected, the interest is calculated directly on the initial loan principal for the entire loan duration:
    Total Interest = Loan Principal × (Flat Rate / 100) × (Duration in Months / 12).
    This total interest is divided equally across all months, keeping both the monthly principal repayment and interest portion identical for every single installment.
  • Indicative Effective Rate (Reducing Balance): When Effective Rate is selected without a Fixed Amount, the interest is calculated daily on the outstanding principal balance:
    Monthly Interest = Outstanding Balance × (Effective Rate / 100) × (Days in Month / 365).
    The monthly installment (EMI) is estimated using the standard reducing balance formula. As the outstanding principal reduces month-by-month, the interest portion of your monthly payment decreases while the principal portion increases.
  • Fixed Amount Option: By enabling the Fixed Amount checkbox, you can input a manual, constant monthly repayment value (Fixed Amount Value). The system will compute the monthly interest based on the reducing balance, and apply the rest of the fixed amount directly to pay off the principal:
    Monthly Principal Repayment = Fixed Amount Value - Monthly Interest.

Generate Amortization Schedule

Click Compute Schedule to generate the principal, interest, and monthly installment breakdown along with remaining outstanding principal.

Post Journal Entries

Click Create Entry to automatically generate and validate accounting entries with corresponding deferred interest.
